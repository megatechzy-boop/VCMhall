Wedding Hall in Nigdi, Pune

Late Venutai Chavan Multipurpose Hall is a wedding venue in Nigdi, Pune. Its air-conditioned Big Hall is listed for up to 1,200 guests, with a Small Hall for up to 350, separate dining spaces and up to eight A/C guest rooms. Ask the team to match your guest count and schedule to the spaces.

Which hall may fit your wedding?

For a large wedding guest list, start by asking about the Big Hall and the event layout. For a smaller gathering, compare the Small Hall capacity with your expected attendance. The listed dining spaces seat 400 and 50 guests at a time; ask how meal service can fit around your ceremony and programme.
