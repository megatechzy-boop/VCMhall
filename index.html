Questions

Common Questions.(सामान्य प्रश्न)

Straight answers based on what the venue offers. For anything else, contact the venue team.

What events can be hosted?

Weddings, engagements, receptions, birthdays, naming ceremonies, family functions, corporate events and social gatherings — plus other celebrations.

What is the maximum capacity?

Big Hall up to 1200 guests · Small Hall up to 350 · Dining Hall up to 400 · VIP A/C Dining up to 50 · up to 8 A/C guest rooms. All spaces are fully air-conditioned.

Do you provide dining facilities?

Yes — a dedicated Dining Hall for 400 at a time, VIP A/C Dining for 50 at a time, and catering support so your caterer can work on site. Menu and pricing are handled with your caterer; contact the venue team for current arrangements.

Are the halls air-conditioned?

Yes. All halls, dining spaces and guest rooms are fully air-conditioned.

Are guest rooms available?

Yes — up to 8 air-conditioned guest rooms for family members and invitees staying over. Ask about room allocation when you enquire.

Is parking available?

On-site parking is available at the venue. For current details on vehicle counts and arrangements, please contact the venue team.

Are projector and LED screen facilities available?

Yes — the venue lists an acoustic system, LED screen, projector and Wi-Fi connectivity. Discuss your AV requirements with the team when you plan your event.

How can I enquire about a date?

Use the Check Availability form or call the venue team on 9359567494. This is an enquiry — your date is confirmed only after venue confirmation.

Can I visit the venue?

Yes. Schedule a venue visit and our team will walk you through the spaces. Working hours: Mon – Sun, 9:00 AM – 10:00 PM.

How is booking confirmed?

An enquiry is not a booking. After you enquire, the venue team contacts you with availability and next steps; your date is confirmed only after venue confirmation. For current policies, please contact the venue team.
